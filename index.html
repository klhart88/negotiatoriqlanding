THE CLIENT BENEFIT

Look beyond the offer price. Understand the impact.

Two offers with similar prices can produce very different outcomes. NegotiatorIQ helps organize the financial details so you can evaluate the tradeoffs that matter to your goals—not just the headline number.

01 / COMPARE

See the options side by side

03 / DECIDE

HOW NEGOTIATORIQ WORKS

Personalized analysis for both sides of the transaction.

We review the relevant offer details, model appropriate scenarios, and walk through the estimated financial consequences together.

For buyers

Explore down payment and closing cash, financing costs, seller credits, and how mortgage buydowns may affect payments today and later.

For sellers

Compare competing offers using estimated mortgage payoff, transaction costs, concessions, and net proceeds—not just sale price.

For the conversation

Identify meaningful differences, examine changing assumptions, and clarify which questions to take to your lender, title company, or other professionals.

Results depend on entered assumptions and are estimates, not lending approvals, guaranteed proceeds, or recommendations to accept a particular offer.
